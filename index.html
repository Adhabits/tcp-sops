Executive Summary

Hifzurrahman's agency faces a critical juncture: maintaining clients while losing money, with no systematic processes, undefined roles, and excessive founder dependency. This blueprint outlines the path from loss-making generalist to ₹10 crore performance marketing powerhouse.

Current Situation

7-8 low-budget local clients (cafés, gym, salon, real estate) generating insufficient revenue to cover ₹28K monthly office expenses. Founders trapped in execution with no role clarity or SOPs.

Strategic Shift

Move from ₹10-20K local clients to ₹50K+ mid-market retainers, focusing on Meta Ads performance marketing with systematic processes and clear role definitions.
